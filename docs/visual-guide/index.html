    How to read the screen

    GitFlux has three columns: your agent on the left, the map in the middle, and "is my work safe?" on the right. These are the pieces the map is built from.

    Every icon

    GitFlux uses Phosphor icons in their two-tone (duotone) style. The background colour of an icon tells you how serious it is: green safe, blue information, amber needs a look, red could lose work, purple GitHub.

    Every animation

    Motion only ever means something happened. If your computer is set to reduce motion, all of it switches off and the screen just updates.
